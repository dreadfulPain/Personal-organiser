Read in a school calendar

Paste it, or open the PDF. Any line with a date on it is picked up; anything without one is left alone. Nothing is guessed about what a date means — you say, once, and it goes in.

A holiday is usually two lines, a start and an end. Tick runs on to the next on the first of them and it becomes every day between; it'll tell you how many before you keep it.

…or open a file:

Year, for any line that doesn't say one: Taken from the document. A table that puts the year on its own line leaves the dates without one, and being twelve months out is the sort of mistake nothing else would show you.

Month the grid is showing: Worked out from which weekday its first column is and how long the month runs. Change it if it's wrong.

And mark the day the students come back as lessons start. A timetable typed in with no dates on it runs for ever — through the set-up week, through the holidays and through next July — so this is the line that tells it when the teaching actually happens.

Book time off

Days you won't be doing anything — away, resting, busy with something else. Nothing gets planned into them, and everything else moves to fit around them. Telling it early is the whole point.
