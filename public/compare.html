One call vs. the pipeline

Paste the same thing through both and see what each one actually finds. This page isn't in the tab bar on purpose — it's a workbench, not a room.

What to look for. Not speed — the pipeline is slower and always will be. Look at whether the single call quietly missed something. Count the items each side found, then read the fragments list: every one of those is a piece of your text, so anything real that appears in the fragments and in neither result is a genuine miss.

It's entirely possible the single call is fine for short pastes and the pipeline only earns its cost on long ones. If that's what you see, set PIPELINE_MIN_CHARS in your .env to roughly where it starts to matter.

Keep the awkward ones

When a real paste catches something out, keep it. A handful of genuine examples — a chat thread, an email chain, a one-paragraph brain-dump — is worth more than any amount of guessing about what the model does.
