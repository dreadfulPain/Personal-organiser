What this adds up to

Counts of what you actually recorded — nothing here is a judgement of the teaching, and none of it knows what a good lesson looks like. It's the things that are hard to see from the inside.

Targets covered

The targets you teach against

Paste your syllabus in. One target a line — a code, then the words. When you change school or year group, paste the new one over the top: the lessons keep their codes and nothing else has to change. Nothing here is ever recalled from memory by the app; matching is done against these words and only these words.

What your headings are called

The app finds these in your plan. Change them when your template changes, when you change year group, or when you change school — nothing else has to change with them. Separated by commas, and spelling slips are matched anyway.
