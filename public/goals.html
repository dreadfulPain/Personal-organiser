Goals & milestones

Name a goal in a sentence — with smart sorting on, the app suggests small milestones you can tweak. Each goal fills a bar toward its next one — short, reachable, and worth a little celebration.

Ask Claude, or anyone, for a plan. Change the bits it got wrong about your situation. Paste the whole thing here — headings, bullets, times in brackets, a deadline written however you like. Nothing gets invented and nothing gets thrown away.
